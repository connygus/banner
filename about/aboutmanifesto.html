The ProRealAlgos Manifesto.

You already know what this kind of trading is, and what it isn't. No Lamborghinis, no jets, no gurus, just 20 algorithms I built for myself and my family because I wanted time away from the screens. The returns were a side effect.

I believe trading should be mechanical and boring. I believe it shouldn't try to beat the market every month, it should survive the bear years and compound through the bull ones. And I believe in a few of us, not thousands, trading the same algos on the same broker, losing together and winning together. Not a quick win. Net worth, compounded over a decade. This is the Capital Climbing Club.
